Frontier is a single platform that lets you hold BTC, ETH and stablecoins alongside fractional positions in real-world asset programs. Your Bitcoin allocation powers daily trading liquidity, while the real-world allocation generates contracted yield — both visible in one portfolio dashboard.
You can start with $1,000 at the Starter level. As your commitment grows, you unlock lower platform fees, higher yield bands and additional privileges. Levels are: Starter $1K, Core $5K, Premium $25K, Private $100K, Black $250K by invitation. Every tier gives access to the full product catalogue.
Digital assets sit in insured, segregated cold storage with 99.99% uptime. Real-world positions are held in SPVs with independent custodians and audited quarterly. Both are reportable from your dashboard and can be withdrawn or transferred subject to the underlying contract terms.
Registration is instant, but accounts enter a short compliance review — usually under 24 hours. You'll receive an email confirming your identity check, then a second email with a secure activation link. You can contact onboarding@frontier.example if you'd like a status update.
Crypto positions settle same-day and can be withdrawn within two minutes of a sell order. Real-world assets depend on the underlying contract — most managed fleet and energy positions offer a rolling secondary market with same-day liquidity; some long-horizon positions may have a short notice period, disclosed before you invest.
A transparent platform fee on assets under management, tiered from 0.50% at Starter down to 0.12% at Black. Trading spreads on BTC average 0.04%. There are no hidden subscription or custody fees. Full fee schedule available in the platform documents.
