Capability

Three verticals. One access vehicle.

Rather than forcing a choice between launch, orbit and ground, the Frontier vehicle spreads contracted exposure across the full stack — so a delay in one vertical doesn't define the whole position.

Launch infrastructure

Contracted capacity across reusable heavy-lift platforms, plus the ground infrastructure that keeps cadence climbing.

  • Reusability contracts & refurbishment
  • Range & pad infrastructure
  • Propellant and logistics chain

Orbital communications

Capacity rights on low-earth-orbit constellations, with revenue tied to subscriber growth and enterprise backhaul demand.

  • LEO capacity rights
  • Enterprise & maritime backhaul
  • Ground-station leases

Energy systems

Grid-scale storage deployments and the software layer that dispatches them — the quiet compounder underneath the whole stack.

  • Utility-scale storage projects
  • Dispatch & arbitrage software
  • Long-duration R&D pipeline
Allocation

Weighted toward the bottleneck.

Allocation isn't fixed. The vehicle rebalances quarterly toward whichever layer of the stack is constraining throughput — historically, launch capacity and ground infrastructure.

Process

From enquiry to allocation in four steps.

No cold outreach, no pressure cycles. Every mandate moves through the same gated process.

01

Qualification

Confirm accreditation and mandate size. You receive the full data room within one business day — nothing gated behind a call.

02

Diligence

Direct access to the investment team, the underlying contract ledger, and the third-party audit trail. Ask anything.

03

Subscription

Digital subscription and KYC. Capital is drawn once the allocation window closes, never held in limbo.

04

Reporting

Quarterly statements, live telemetry dashboard, and an annual in-person or virtual review with the managers.
