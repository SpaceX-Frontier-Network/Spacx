Questions

Answers, up front

Everything you need to evaluate the vehicle. Nothing buried in a footnote.

You are purchasing limited partnership interests in the Frontier Network Fund, a regulated investment vehicle that holds ring-fenced positions across three asset classes: launch infrastructure contracts, orbital broadband revenue shares, and grid-scale energy storage. Each position is held by a qualified custodian and reconciled daily.

Positions are marked to model monthly using discounted cash flow against contracted revenue, then reconciled quarterly by an independent administrator. Physical assets are appraised annually by a third-party aerospace valuation firm. Every valuation memo is available to LPs on request.

Redemption windows open quarterly with a 30-day notice period for Observer tier and 15-day notice for Operator and above. An internal matching book allows accelerated secondary transfers between existing LPs, typically settling within 10 business days.

The fund is open to accredited investors in the United States and qualified purchasers in select international jurisdictions. Minimums start at $500 for the Observer tier. Full eligibility requirements are outlined in the private placement memorandum, available after a short qualification call.

Observer tier carries no management fee and a 1.5% performance carry above an 8% hurdle. Operator tier is 0.85% management with 1.0% carry. Mission Partner structures are negotiated individually. There are no entry fees, exit fees, or hidden operational charges.

Assets are held with a qualified custodian in segregated accounts, never commingled with fund operating capital. The fund maintains D&O and errors-and-omissions coverage, and all positions are independently reconciled daily. Quarterly financials are audited by a big-four firm.
